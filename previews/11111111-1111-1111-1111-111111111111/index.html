About Serenity

Bodywork that actually listens to your body.

Serenity Massage Therapy is a calm, one-on-one studio in St. Louis built around a simple idea: every body carries tension differently, so every session should be different too.

Whether you're working through a stubborn knot, easing the aches of pregnancy, or just need an hour to exhale, your therapist tailors pressure, pacing, and technique to what you actually need that day.

Licensed, insured, and genuinely unhurried — no upsells, no rushing you out the door.
