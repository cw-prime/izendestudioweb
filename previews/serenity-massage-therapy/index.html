About Serenity

Bodywork that listens before it works.

Serenity Massage Therapy is built around a simple promise: no two bodies carry stress the same way, so no two sessions should feel copied and pasted.

Whether you are working through shoulder tension, pregnancy aches, or the kind of stress that sits in your jaw, your therapist adapts pressure, pacing, and technique to what you actually need that day.

The experience is intentionally unhurried: quiet intake, clear pressure checks, warm table setup, and practical aftercare so the relief lasts longer than the drive home.

No upsell energy.Just focused bodywork, honest recommendations, and space to exhale.
