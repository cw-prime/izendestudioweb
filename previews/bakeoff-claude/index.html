Our Story

Healing Touch, Right Here in St. Louis

Welcome to Serenity Massage Therapy — a calm, welcoming space where you can finally let your shoulders drop. I'm a licensed massage therapist with a deep belief that regular bodywork isn't a luxury; it's an essential part of feeling your best and living fully.

Whether you're carrying the tension of a demanding week, managing a specific area of chronic discomfort, or expecting a little one and craving some safe, nurturing care, every session is shaped around you. I take the time to listen, adapt my technique, and make sure you leave feeling genuinely better — not just temporarily relaxed.

Booking is easy, the space is inviting, and I keep my schedule manageable so you always receive my full, unhurried attention. I can't wait to welcome you.

What We Offer

Massage for Every Need

Each session is personalized to address your body's unique needs — no two visits are exactly alike.

💆

Deep Tissue Massage

Targeted, firm pressure to release chronic tension held deep in your muscles and connective tissue. Ideal for persistent pain, stiffness, and postural imbalances built up over time.

60 · 90 · 120 min available
🌿

Relaxation Massage

A flowing, gentle full-body session designed to quiet your nervous system, melt away stress, and leave you feeling refreshed and deeply at ease. Perfect for first-time clients.

60 · 90 min available
🤱

Prenatal Massage

Safe, nurturing bodywork for expectant mothers in their second and third trimesters. Relieves back pain, swelling, and fatigue while supporting a healthy, comfortable pregnancy.

60 min — specially positioned
🖐️

Targeted Spot Treatment

A focused 30-minute session for one specific area of concern — neck and shoulders, lower back, or feet and calves. Great as a stand-alone or paired with another service.

30 min focused session
🔥

Add to any 60+ min session
